# 🔍 Business Insights

## 1️⃣ United Kingdom Dominates Revenue

The United Kingdom contributed the largest share of total business revenue, significantly outperforming all other countries.

### 📌 Business Impact:
- The UK market should remain the primary business focus.
- More advertising and inventory investment can increase profits further.

---

## 2️⃣ Revenue Shows Seasonal Trends

Revenue fluctuates across different months, with some periods showing major spikes.

### 📌 Business Impact:
- Seasonal sales campaigns can maximize revenue.
- Inventory planning should align with peak demand periods.

---

## 3️⃣ Best-Selling Products Drive Major Sales

Products such as:
- WORLD WAR items
- JUMBO BAG products
- ASSORTED collections

generated the highest quantity sold.

### 📌 Business Impact:
- High-demand products should be restocked regularly.
- Businesses can introduce similar product variants.

---

## 4️⃣ Customer Distribution is Region-Focused

Most customers belong to:
- United Kingdom
- Germany
- France

### 📌 Business Impact:
- Regional targeting strategies can improve customer engagement.
- Expanding into similar markets may increase business growth.

---

## 5️⃣ Mid-Range Products Perform Better

Products with medium pricing contributed significantly to sales volume.

### 📌 Business Impact:
- Customers prefer affordable products with value.
- Pricing strategies should focus on competitive mid-range pricing.
